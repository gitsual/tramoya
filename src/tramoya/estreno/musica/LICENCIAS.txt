ascensor.ogg
============

Lobby music for the premiere: 34 bars of bossa (F major, 112 bpm) that loop without a seam. Composed and
played by the orchestra of cronica (https://github.com/gitsual/cronica, MIT): the composition is MIT like
Tramoya; the instrument samples it is played with come from the two libraries below.

Música de ascensor para el estreno: 34 compases de bossa que dan la vuelta sin costura. Compuesta y tocada por la
